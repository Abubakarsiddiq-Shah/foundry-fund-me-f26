**Compiling:** Use `forge build` or `forge compile` to turn your smart contract
 code into machine-readable bytecode.

**Testing:** Use `forge test` to execute your test scripts (typically files ending
 in `.t.sol`) and verify your contract logic.

**Deploying:** Use `forge script script/ContractName.s.sol` to run your deployment
 scripts. For live or test networks, you must include the `--broadcast` flag to submit
  the transactions.

**Note on Network Connectivity:**
Using the `--rpc-url` flag is critical because it connects your local environment to the 
specific blockchain network you are targeting. This allows your scripts to read the
network's current state and correctly interact with existing addresses, such as price 
feeds or other deployed infrastructure.
